How wide the camera sees. Keeps the path lined up with the real room as you turn your head.

Calibrate: lay the beacon flat, lay the phone face up on top of it (top edge along the IMU's printed Y arrow) and leave both still. After a 2-second countdown it calibrates by itself. That spot becomes the origin (0, 0, 0): X right, Y along the arrow, Z up.
From then on the beacon and the phone each track their own position. The beacon shows as a live dot with a trail, and /graph on the laptop shows both in 3D.
Position comes from integrating motion sensors, so it drifts. To reset it, touch the beacon to the phone and hold the screen. Tap clears the trail.
Laptop keys: K calibrate, Z re-zero, C clear.
